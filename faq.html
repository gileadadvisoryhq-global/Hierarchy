Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, and care

How do I order furniture from Homefront?

You can submit an enquiry directly through our website using the 'Enquire' buttons, call our sales representative at 080 667 23 960, or visit our physical showroom in Lekki, Lagos.

Do you deliver outside Lekki / Lagos State?

Yes, we deliver across all major areas in Lagos and coordinate logistics for nationwide delivery across Nigeria.

Can I request custom fabric colors or dimensions?

Yes, custom orders are available for selected upholstered furniture items. Contact our team with your exact specifications.
