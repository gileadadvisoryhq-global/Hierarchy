About Homefront Interiors

Delivering high-quality, product-focused furniture solutions in Lagos

Homefront Interiors and Furniture is a premiere furniture retailer operating out of Ikota Shopping Complex in Lekki, Lagos. We specialise in offering beautifully designed, durable, and functional interior furniture tailored for residences, corporate offices, and hospitality settings across Nigeria.

We bridge the gap between design aesthetic and long-lasting utility. Every piece in our collection—from full living room sectionals to executive desk spaces—is selected with strong attention to material quality, upholstery endurance, and modern ergonomics.
